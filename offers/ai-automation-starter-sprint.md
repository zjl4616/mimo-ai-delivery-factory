# Offer: AI Automation Starter Sprint

## Promise

In 3 days, turn one repetitive business workflow into a working AI-assisted prototype with documentation and a maintenance checklist.

## Best Customers

- small business owners
- foreign trade operators
- community operators
- content teams
- solo founders
- course creators

## Strong Fit Problems

- repeated inquiry replies
- customer data cleanup
- daily or weekly report generation
- content drafting and repurposing
- Feishu/Sheets-based operations
- internal knowledge-base cleanup

## Scope

### Day 1: Diagnose

- interview or written intake
- map the current workflow
- identify repeated steps
- score automation opportunities
- select one prototype target

### Day 2: Build

- implement the prototype
- connect data sources where available
- generate draft outputs
- add a human review step
- record assumptions and limitations

### Day 3: Handoff

- test with sample data
- document the workflow
- write maintenance checklist
- prepare a short handoff guide
- recommend next sprint or retainer

## Deliverables

- workflow map
- automation scorecard
- working prototype or runnable script
- handoff guide
- maintenance checklist
- next-step roadmap

## Pricing Ladder

- Audit only: RMB 499 to 999
- Starter Sprint: RMB 1,999 to 4,999
- Implementation Sprint: RMB 6,999 to 19,999
- Monthly Maintenance: RMB 1,999 to 9,999

## Sales Hook

If a process happens every day, follows similar steps, and burns founder or staff time, it is a candidate for automation. Start with one workflow, not the whole company.
