# DM Scripts

## Warm Contact

I am packaging a small AI automation sprint.

The idea is simple: pick one repeated workflow, map it, build a prototype, keep human review where it matters, and hand it over with docs.

If you have one task that repeats every day or week, send me one sample input and the output you want. I can score whether it is worth automating.

## Founder Or Operator

I noticed your work seems to involve repeated operations and customer-facing tasks.

I am offering a narrow AI automation sprint: one workflow, one prototype, documented handoff. No giant platform build first.

If there is one recurring task your team still handles manually, I can score whether it is a good automation candidate.

## Course Creator

I am testing a workflow service for creators: turn one piece of source material into reusable posts, summaries, student replies, or lesson assets, with human review before publishing.

If you have a repeated content or student-ops task, send one sample input and the desired output. I can suggest a small prototype scope.

## Developer Or AI Builder

I am packaging an AI coding workflow setup for founders and builders who already use coding agents but feel the output gets messy.

The deliverable is a repeatable repo workflow: specs, agent instructions, review gates, test checklist, and handoff docs.

If you have a repo or project that feels hard to control with AI coding, I can do a quick workflow diagnosis.

## Reply When They Ask "What Can You Automate?"

Good first candidates:

- inquiry replies
- weekly reports
- content repurposing
- lead qualification
- data cleanup
- internal FAQ or knowledge retrieval
- AI coding workflow and review process

The best first sprint is one workflow with clear inputs and outputs, not a full-company AI rebuild.
