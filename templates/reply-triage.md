# Reply Triage

Use this after a lead replies.

## Strong Fit

Signals:

- real repeated workflow
- clear sample input
- desired output is visible
- buyer accepts human review
- budget or urgency exists

Action:

1. Fill `automation-scorecard.md`.
2. Draft proposal with `proposal.md`.
3. Ask human operator to confirm final price and payment terms.

## Medium Fit

Signals:

- interest but no workflow detail
- pain is real but vague
- multiple possible workflows

Action:

Ask:

- What task repeats most often?
- Can you send one sample input and expected output?
- Who reviews the output today?

## Weak Fit

Signals:

- wants "an AI system" without a workflow
- wants guaranteed revenue
- no data access
- no review owner

Action:

Narrow scope to one workflow or pause.

## Reject

Signals:

- wants raw API/token resale
- requests spam, scraping without permission, or impersonation
- wants unreviewed sensitive messages sent automatically
- asks for false claims or fake testimonials

Action:

Decline the risky part and redirect to a legitimate implementation service.
