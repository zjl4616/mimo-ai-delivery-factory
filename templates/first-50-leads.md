## Daily Rule

- Add 10 leads.
- Send 10 low-risk first messages.
- Follow up only twice.
- Draft proposals only for leads who send a real workflow sample.

## Minimum Lead Fields

- name
- channel
- public profile or contact URL
- likely repeated workflow
- first message date
- reply status
- fit score
- next follow-up date
