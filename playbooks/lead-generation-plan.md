# Lead Generation Plan

The goal is to turn the MiMo Token Plan into paid delivery capacity before 2026-06-27.

## Target Buyers

| Segment | Pain | First offer |
|---|---|---|
| Foreign trade sellers | repeated inquiry replies and product matching | inquiry reply automation |
| Course creators | content repurposing and student operations | content workflow sprint |
| Small agencies | weekly reporting and client updates | report automation |
| Community operators | onboarding, FAQ, and member tagging | community ops workflow |
| Solo founders | scattered manual operations | automation starter sprint |
| AI coding learners | messy AI-built projects | AI coding workflow setup |

## Daily Acquisition Loop

1. Find 10 leads from warm network, groups, GitHub, blogs, or public business profiles.
2. Record each lead in `templates/lead-tracker.csv`.
3. Send a low-pressure workflow audit message.
4. If they reply, ask for one sample input/output.
5. Score the workflow using `templates/automation-scorecard.md`.
6. Send a proposal only after a concrete workflow is visible.
7. Stop after two unanswered follow-ups.

## Fit Score

Score 1-5 for each:

- repeats weekly or daily
- time cost is visible
- input data is accessible
- output can be judged
- human review is acceptable
- buyer has authority or budget

Strong fit: 22+

Medium fit: 16-21

Weak fit: below 16
