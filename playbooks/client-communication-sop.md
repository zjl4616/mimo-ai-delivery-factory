# Client Communication SOP

Use this SOP when I communicate with leads or customers on the operator's behalf.

## What I Can Send Directly

- Low-risk first outreach to warm contacts or public business accounts.
- Follow-up questions about workflow details, sample inputs, tools, and timing.
- Discovery-call summaries and automation scorecards.
- Draft scopes based on approved offer templates.
- Delivery updates, handoff notes, and maintenance reminders.

## What Needs Human Confirmation

- Final price, discount, refund, or payment-term changes.
- Contract acceptance, invoice sending, and payment collection.
- Claims about revenue, guaranteed results, or customer outcomes.
- Use of a real client name, private screenshot, private data, or testimonial.
- Any message that could create legal, financial, or employment obligations.

## Reply Triage

| Signal | Meaning | Next action |
|---|---|---|
| Sends a real workflow and sample | Strong fit | Score it and draft a starter sprint proposal |
| Says "interested" but no detail | Medium fit | Ask for one repeated task and one sample input/output |
| Wants a huge vague AI system | Risky fit | Narrow to one workflow first |
| Asks for token/API resale | Reject | Offer implementation service instead |
| Requests unreviewed external sending | Risky | Keep human review in scope |
| No reply after 2 follow-ups | Cold | Stop outreach |

## First Outreach

Hi, I am packaging a small AI automation service.

The scope is intentionally narrow: pick one repeated workflow, map it, build a prototype, keep human review where it matters, and hand it over with docs.

If you have one task that happens every day or week and wastes time, send me one example input and the output you want. I can score whether it is worth automating.

## If They Reply With Interest

Great. To avoid turning this into a vague AI project, I only need one workflow first:

- What task repeats every day or week?
- Who handles it now?
- How long does one cycle take?
- What is one real sample input?
- What should the output look like?
- What must remain human-reviewed?

I will use that to give you a quick automation scorecard and a small prototype scope.

## If They Ask For Price

For the first version I usually keep it as a fixed-scope starter sprint.

Typical range:

- RMB 1,999-4,999 for one workflow prototype
- RMB 999-12,999 for an AI coding workflow setup, depending on repo and team complexity

I will confirm the exact fixed fee after seeing the workflow, sample input/output, and access requirements.

## If They Want A Call

Happy to do a short workflow diagnosis call. The goal is not to brainstorm every AI idea; it is to pick one repeated task with clear inputs, outputs, and review points.

Before the call, please send:

- one workflow candidate
- one sample input
- the expected output
- tools involved
- any data that must stay private

## Follow-Up 1

Quick follow-up. If you still want to test this, send one repeated workflow and one sample input/output. I will tell you whether it is a good automation candidate before proposing any build.

## Follow-Up 2

Closing the loop here. I will pause unless you send a workflow sample later. The best fit is a task that repeats weekly or daily, has clear input data, and still benefits from human review before anything external is sent.
