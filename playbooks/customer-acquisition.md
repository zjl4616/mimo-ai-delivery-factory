# Customer Acquisition Playbook

## Positioning

Do not sell "AI tokens" or "AI magic". Sell a small operational outcome:

> I help you automate one repeated workflow first, keep human review where it matters, and hand over something your team can actually use.

## Best First Channels

### Warm Network

Use for first revenue. Send to founders, operators, course creators, agency owners, and people already talking about AI tools.

Goal: 20 direct messages in 2 days.

### GitHub

Use as public proof. Publish this repository, create issues for public roadmap, and show real templates and case studies.

Goal: credibility, not random inbound at first.

### WeChat / Feishu Groups

Use where the account is already trusted. Share a practical diagnostic checklist, not a sales pitch.

Goal: invite people to submit one repeated workflow for scoring.

### Xiaohongshu / Video Accounts / Blog

Use content from the Obsidian vault. The strongest posts are practical before/after stories:

- "How to judge whether a task is worth automating"
- "The smallest AI automation stack: n8n + AI + spreadsheet"
- "Why your AI coding workflow becomes messy"
- "One workflow first, not the whole company"

## Outreach Rules

- No mass spam.
- No fake personalization.
- No promise of guaranteed revenue.
- No unapproved use of private customer examples.
- Ask for one workflow and one sample input, not a big meeting.
- Send proposals only after a clear fit signal.

## Warm DM Script

I am packaging a small AI automation service: pick one repeated business workflow, map it, build a prototype, and hand it over with docs.  

If you have one task that happens every day or week and wastes staff time, send me one example. I can score whether it is worth automating and suggest a small prototype scope.

## Group Post

I am testing a fixed-scope AI automation sprint. The idea is simple: do not automate the whole company, just pick one repeated workflow first.

Good candidates:

- customer inquiry replies
- weekly reports
- content repurposing
- customer data cleanup
- Feishu/Sheets-based operations

If you send one repeated workflow plus one sample input/output, I can give a quick automation scorecard.

## Agent Communication Boundary

I can draft and send low-risk outreach, follow-up questions, scorecards, proposal drafts, delivery updates, and handoff messages.

Pause for human confirmation before final pricing, payment terms, contract acceptance, real customer claims, testimonials, publishing private data, or any promise that creates legal or financial obligations.
