# 30-Day Token-To-Cash Playbook

Window: 2026-05-27 to 2026-06-27.

## Principle

Do not try to consume tokens. Convert model capacity into proof, trust, and fixed-scope paid work.

## Week 1: Publish Proof

- publish this GitHub repository
- publish the static landing page
- write 2 public case studies
- create 1 lead magnet checklist
- prepare 3 outreach scripts

Goal: make it easy for a stranger to understand what you sell.

## Week 2: Outreach

- send 50 targeted messages
- offer 10 free workflow diagnoses
- post 5 pieces of content
- turn every useful reply into a refined FAQ

Goal: book conversations.

## Week 3: Close Starter Sprints

- run diagnosis calls or async audits
- send fixed-scope proposals within 24 hours
- close 2 to 3 starter sprints
- build only one workflow per client

Goal: collect first revenue and real cases.

## Week 4: Package Repeatability

- turn delivered work into sanitized case studies
- create before/after screenshots
- write maintenance offer
- ask for referrals
- create a higher-ticket implementation sprint

Goal: move from one-off work to repeatable service.

## Daily Operating Rhythm

- 60 minutes: prospecting and follow-up
- 90 minutes: public asset or content creation
- 3 to 5 hours: delivery work
- 30 minutes: case-study and template extraction

## Outreach Targets

- foreign trade sellers
- course creators
- community operators
- small agencies
- local service businesses
- founders who already use AI tools

## First Message

I am building small AI automation workflows for repetitive business tasks. If you have one task that happens every day and wastes staff time, I can map it and show whether it is worth automating. No big system, just one workflow first.

## Follow-Up

The best first automation is usually not the flashiest one. It is the workflow that repeats often, has clear inputs, and still benefits from human review. If you send me one example, I can score it and suggest a small prototype scope.
