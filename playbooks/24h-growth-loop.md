# 24H MiMo Growth Loop

Goal: keep MiMo turning available model capacity into revenue-facing assets every hour until 2026-06-27.

## Operating Rule

Do not burn tokens for vanity output. Every run must create or improve one of:

- a lead list
- an outreach message
- a public content asset
- a proposal draft
- a delivery template
- a demo or case study
- a follow-up or objection response
- a decision log that tells what to double down on

## Hourly Loop

1. Inspect `generated/execution-log.md` and the latest experiment files.
2. Pick the highest-leverage next action.
3. Use MiMo through `scripts/mimo_chat.ps1` when drafting, analyzing, or expanding assets.
4. Save outputs under `generated/`, `experiments/`, `content/`, or `leads/`.
5. Mark anything requiring the operator as `NEEDS_USER_CONFIRMATION`.
6. End with the next action and what signal would justify scaling.

## Daily Themes

| Time block | Primary job | Output |
|---|---|---|
| 00:00-08:00 Beijing | high-token drafting during lower-cost window | content, proposals, demos, lead magnets |
| 08:00-12:00 Beijing | lead research and scoring | lead rows, pain hypotheses |
| 12:00-18:00 Beijing | outreach preparation and reply handling | DM drafts, reply triage, proposals |
| 18:00-24:00 Beijing | delivery assets and recap | templates, case studies, decision log |

## Confirmation Gates

Only the operator confirms:

- final price
- payment link or invoice
- contract acceptance
- sending a message from a personal account if identity or relationship matters
- publishing a real customer name, screenshot, or claim
- using private customer data in public material
- buying tools, domains, ads, or subscriptions

## Scale Criteria

Double down on a route when it has at least one of:

- paid order
- prepayment intent
- two qualified replies from ten targeted messages
- one real workflow sample from a buyer
- repeatable delivery asset that can be reused for more than one client

Stop or pause a route when:

- no reply after 20 targeted messages
- workflow requires risky unreviewed external sending
- buyer only wants token/API resale
- delivery requires access that is unavailable
- legal or financial exposure is unclear
